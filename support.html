Why didn't the orb empty when I logged a meal?

The orb is your glycogen tank. Eating fills it; moving or fasting drains it. A single meal is a small share of the tank, so the level rises a notch rather than emptying — it only visibly drains over hours of deficit. That's the physics working, not a bug.

Why is my “LB TO GO” number a starting estimate?

Reserve learns your real metabolism over time. Until you've logged a few days and weighed in, the number is your goal distance as an honest starting point. Log meals and add weigh-ins and it calibrates to you — that's the whole idea.

Does my data leave my phone?

No. Reserve has no server and no account. Your data stays on your device (with an optional private iCloud mirror you control). See the Privacy Policy for details.
