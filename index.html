The whole idea

Always know how close the next pound is.

Every other app hands you a number and walks away. RESERVE shows you your position — the exact gap between you and the next pound, closing in real time.

  • A finish line, not a scoreboard. "2,540 kcal to the next pound down — about 3 days at your pace" gets you off the couch. "You weigh 184" never did.
  • The ring is the pound you're on. Around the orb, a ring of light drains as you burn fat. You literally watch the gap close.
  • Both directions, honestly. Losing, you see how far to the next pound off. Gaining, you see how much a walk banks before it sticks.
The Orb

One glance. Whole metabolism.

Under the glass is your actual biology: the energy you've eaten, the energy you've burned, and the state your body is in right now.

  • Fuel states, live. Watch yourself move from FED into BURNING FAT — the orb changes as your body does.
  • Your real daily burn. Not a generic formula — RESERVE measures how your body actually spends energy, week after week.
  • Everything counts. Meals, water, walks, coffee, sleep, macros, supplements — logged in seconds, reflected instantly.
  • It learns you. Your staples, your patterns, your rhythm. The longer you use it, the more it speaks your language.
Honest by design
The kindest health app you've ever met. No shame, ever.

RESERVE never scolds, never nags, never punishes a weigh-in. When the scale goes up, it stays respectfully silent — because water masks change, and you deserve an app that knows the difference. It celebrates what's real and says nothing about the noise.
